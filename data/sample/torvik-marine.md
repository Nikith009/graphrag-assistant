# Torvik Marine

Torvik Marine builds battery-electric passenger ferries at its shipyard in Bergen, Norway. Its Torvik 40 ferry carries up to 300 passengers and is in service on several Norwegian fjord routes.

All Torvik 40 ferries use battery packs from Kestrel Energy. After the European Vehicle Safety Agency report in August 2024, Torvik Marine inspected the packs on its fleet and replaced packs on two ferries as a precaution.

Torvik Marine is partly owned by Nordlys Invest, an investment fund based in Oslo.
