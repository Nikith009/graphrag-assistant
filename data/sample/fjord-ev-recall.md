# Fjord EV Recall, 2024

In March 2024 Halvorsen Motors recalled 12,000 Fjord EV vehicles after several reports of battery packs overheating while charging. No injuries were reported.

The European Vehicle Safety Agency opened an investigation into the recall. Its lead investigator, Dr. Ines Kovac, traced the overheating to battery cells from a single production batch with defective separator film. The affected cells had been made by Ardent Cells and built into packs by Kestrel Energy.

The agency's report, published in August 2024, recommended that every company using cells from the affected Ardent Cells batch inspect its products. Kestrel Energy replaced the affected packs at no cost to Halvorsen Motors.

The recall cost Halvorsen Motors an estimated 85 million euros and delayed the launch of its next model by six months.
