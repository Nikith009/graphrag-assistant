# Brightwater Robotics

Brightwater Robotics designs autonomous mobile robots for warehouses. Founded in Trondheim in 2018 by Sofie Lund, the company was acquired by Halvorsen Motors in 2023 and kept its own brand and management.

Brightwater's main external customer is Nordlys Logistics, which runs 120 Brightwater robots across three distribution centers in Sweden. The two companies jointly developed a robot that can lift pallets of up to one tonne.

Brightwater Robotics uses navigation software licensed from Polaris Vision, a computer vision company in Helsinki.
