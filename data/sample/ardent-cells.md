# Ardent Cells

Ardent Cells is a battery cell manufacturer based in Busan, South Korea, led by chief executive Park Ji-ho. It produces cylindrical and pouch cells for electric vehicles and marine applications.

The separator film used in Ardent's cells is supplied by Lumen Polymers, a materials company in Osaka, Japan. After the 2024 Fjord EV recall, Ardent Cells and Lumen Polymers jointly found that a coating machine at Lumen's plant had malfunctioned for two weeks, which produced the defective separator film.

Ardent Cells has since added an automated inspection step for separator film and moved part of its film purchasing to a second supplier, Vesta Materials.
