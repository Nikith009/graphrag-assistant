# Halvorsen Motors

Halvorsen Motors is an electric vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Oslo, Norway. It was founded in 2014 by Erik Halvorsen, who remains chief executive. The company sells three models in Europe; the best seller is the Fjord EV, a mid-size crossover that accounted for about 60 percent of deliveries in 2024.

Halvorsen Motors does not make its own battery packs. Since 2019 every Fjord EV has used packs supplied by Kestrel Energy under a long-term contract. Final assembly takes place at the company's plant in Drammen, which employs around 2,400 people.

In 2023 Halvorsen Motors acquired Brightwater Robotics, a start-up that builds autonomous warehouse robots. Brightwater still operates as a separate brand, and its robots now move parts around the Drammen plant.

The company's main competitor in the Nordic market is Solberg Automotive, which launched a rival crossover in 2024.
