# Kestrel Energy

Kestrel Energy assembles lithium-ion battery packs at a factory in Gdansk, Poland. The company designs the pack housing, cooling system and battery management software itself, but it buys the individual battery cells from outside suppliers.

Most of Kestrel's cells come from Ardent Cells, a South Korean manufacturer. Kestrel signed a supply agreement with Ardent Cells in 2020 and expanded it in 2023 to cover a new high-density cell.

Kestrel Energy's two largest customers are Halvorsen Motors, which uses Kestrel packs in the Fjord EV, and Torvik Marine, which uses them in its electric ferries. Together the two customers account for roughly 70 percent of Kestrel's revenue.

Kestrel's chief technology officer, Marta Nowak, leads the battery management software team.
